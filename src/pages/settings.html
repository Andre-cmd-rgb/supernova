Battery and video

On battery, what moves on screen costs the most power. These apply only while unplugged unless noted.

Video quality on batteryHigher streams cost more to decode and to move around.
Video quality plugged in
Prefer 30 fps video on batteryHalves the frames to draw. On 60 fps videos YouTube may then offer a lower resolution.
Limit page animations to 30 fps on batteryLike Chrome's Energy Saver. Video is not affected.
Freeze hidden tabs afterA frozen tab runs nothing until you come back to it. Pinned tabs and tabs playing sound never sleep.
Unload hidden tabs afterFrees their memory; the page reloads when you return.
